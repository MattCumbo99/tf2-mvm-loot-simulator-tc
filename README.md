A React-based application that lets you simulate the loot experience of playing a typical tour of Two Cities in Mann vs. Machine, without having to actually play the game. Test your luck to see if you should've gotten that australium you really wanted, or see how long it takes you to land the mythical golden frying pan.

Try it out here: https://mattcumbo99.github.io/tf2-mvm-loot-simulator-tc/

Prices of each item are not up to date and are meant more for an overall value. Sheens and killstreaker effects add an estimated cost multiplier and should not be taken as actual price facts. Remember: This project was made just for fun.
